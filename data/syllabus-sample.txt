COURSE DESCRIPTION
An introduction to vector spaces, linear transformations, and matrix theory. The course
develops the algebra of matrices alongside its geometric interpretation, culminating in
eigenvalue problems and diagonalization.

PREREQUISITES
MATH 1210 (Calculus I) or equivalent. Students are expected to be comfortable with high
school algebra: manipulating symbolic expressions, solving linear equations, working with
fractions and signed quantities, and reading function notation such as f(x) and f(g(x)).
Familiarity with plotting points and lines in the coordinate plane is assumed. No prior
exposure to matrices is required.

WEEKLY SCHEDULE

Week 1  — Systems of linear equations. Geometric view: intersecting lines and planes.
          Row operations. We assume you can already solve a linear equation and read a
          slope from an equation of a line.
Week 2  — Gaussian elimination and row echelon form. Existence and uniqueness of
          solutions. Consistent and inconsistent systems.
Week 3  — Vectors in R^n. Vector addition and scalar multiplication. Linear combinations.
          Assumes fluency with signed arithmetic and coordinates.
Week 4  — Span and linear independence. The geometry of spanning sets.
Week 5  — Linear transformations. The matrix of a transformation; columns as images of
          the basis vectors. Assumes you know what it means for a map to be linear, and
          are comfortable composing functions.
Week 6  — Matrix algebra. Matrix multiplication as composition of transformations.
          MIDTERM EXAM 1 (Friday, covers Weeks 1–5)
Week 7  — Inverse matrices. Invertibility criteria. Elementary matrices.
Week 8  — Determinants. 2x2 and general case. Determinant as signed volume scaling.
Week 9  — Vector spaces and subspaces. Column space and null space.
Week 10 — Basis and dimension. Rank–nullity.
Week 11 — Coordinates relative to a basis. Change of basis.
          MIDTERM EXAM 2 (Friday, covers Weeks 6–10)
Week 12 — Similarity of matrices. Similar matrices as one map seen from two bases.
Week 13 — Eigenvalues and eigenvectors. The characteristic polynomial.
Week 14 — Diagonalization and its applications.
Week 15 — Inner products, orthogonality, and projections. Least squares.
Week 16 — Review. FINAL EXAM.
